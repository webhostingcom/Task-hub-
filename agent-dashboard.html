What should I do as an Agent?

Use this dashboard to check your role, wallet, and activity status. Commissions are only counted after eligible activity is verified.

1. Check your role

Read the role details and commission amount below so you know the terms of your Agent program.

2. Check Agent Activity

Review the activity list for instructions or status updates. If it is empty, no activity has been recorded for your account yet.

3. Wait for verification

Pending activities have not been verified. A commission should only be treated as earned after verification.

4. Withdraw from your wallet

When your available wallet balance meets the minimum withdrawal requirement, use Withdraw Funds to submit a request.

Important: This page currently displays activities recorded in Task Hub. It does not create or assign activities itself. If you have no activity and have not received instructions elsewhere, contact the Task Hub administrator for the next eligible activity. Do not pay anyone privately to unlock work.
